This is my final project, a turn-based game in the style of old-school JRPGs like Final Fantasy and Dragon Quest. The goal of the game is to defeat 10 enemies, then kill the final boss. Players can change the difficulty by selectively choosing their party (more or less party members, max 3). Each character type has access to different spells and have different stats. Different actions correspond to different animations for each character type. During exploration, there will be random enemy encounters that will change depending on the region. The background music and combat music will also change depending on the region. Be sure to manage your MP since the final boss is almost impossible without magic. If you're struggling, it's in the dungeon.
